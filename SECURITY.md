# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of our project seriously.

If you discover a security vulnerability, please do NOT report it by creating a public issue. Instead, please use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature to disclose it to us.

To report a vulnerability, go to the "Security" tab of this repository, click on "Advisories", and then click "Report a vulnerability".

We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and we will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions and quickly address the issue.

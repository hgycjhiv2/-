## 2024-05-24 - Missing Security Policy
**Vulnerability:** The repository completely lacks a security policy (`SECURITY.md`), meaning researchers have no documented safe harbor or procedure for reporting vulnerabilities responsibly.
**Learning:** Even empty or newly created repositories need a fundamental security policy to establish reporting procedures before active development begins. Without one, vulnerabilities may be disclosed publicly.
**Prevention:** Always ensure a `SECURITY.md` is initialized at the creation of a repository. Use GitHub's built-in private vulnerability reporting mechanisms instead of boilerplate placeholder text to ensure actual usability.
